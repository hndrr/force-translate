## 生成プロンプト

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a single finished application icon for "Force Translate", a Chrome browser extension that translates webpage text into Japanese. Intended deliverable: a real transparent-background square PNG master, 1024x1024, to be downsampled to 16, 32, 48 and 128 pixels.
Design: one confident compact geometric translation emblem, two slightly overlapping rounded speech cards carrying a very bold clean Latin "A" and Japanese "あ". Make the Japanese あ anatomically correct and recognizable. Use a deep charcoal foreground card and a warm ivory rear card, with a very small vivid orange directional arrow integrated into the overlap to suggest conversion. Thick large glyphs, very simple shapes, strong contrast, flat vector-like finish, balanced optical weight. No gradients, no texture, no 3D, no shadows, no fine details. Not a copy of any existing company's translation logo.
Composition: front-facing, one icon only centered in a square canvas. Entire icon including all cards and arrow fits within the central 75% of the canvas width and height, with generous 12.5% fully transparent margin on all four sides. The two cards form the icon silhouette; no surrounding app tile or enclosing background square. Genuine alpha transparency outside the emblem, not a checkerboard drawn into the image. No brand name, no extra wording, no mockup, no border, no grid, no watermark. Emphasize clear legibility at tiny browser-toolbar sizes.
